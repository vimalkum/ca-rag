Interpretation Table: Defines key legal and technical terms (e.g., Data Controller, GDPR, DPIA) used throughout the agreement.
Glossary: Lists acronyms and abbreviations to help readers understand shortened terms.
Data Sharing Agreement (Parties & Roles): Identifies the parties involved, their addresses, and assigns the Lead Agency.
Evaluation for a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA): Assesses whether the data sharing poses high risks to individuals and if a DPIA is legally required.
Purpose of the Data Sharing: Explains why data is being shared and links it to specific public functions or services.
Data to be Shared: Details the exact personal and non-personal data fields being exchanged.
Function of the Parties: Clarifies each party’s role and responsibility in relation to the shared data.
Legal Basis: Sets out the legal grounds (GDPR articles, Acts, Regulations) that justify the data sharing.
Impetus for Data Sharing: Describes the practical or policy need driving the data exchange.
Categories of Data Shared: Classifies data types (e.g., identifiers, contact data, educational data).
Duration and Frequency: States how long the agreement lasts and how often data will be shared.
How Data Will Be Processed: Explains handling procedures, workflows, and processing steps once data is received.
Restrictions: Lists limits on data use, onward sharing, and purpose limitation.
Security Measures: Outlines technical and organisational safeguards protecting the data.
Retention: Specifies how long data will be kept before deletion.
Methods Used to Destroy/Delete Data: Describes secure deletion or destruction processes.
Withdrawal from Agreement: Explains how a party can exit the agreement and consequences of withdrawal.
Other Matters: Covers miscellaneous legal clauses (governing law, amendments, audits).
Schedule A – Data Protection Impact Assessment: Provides the DPIA summary or detailed assessment.
Schedule B: Contains supplementary operational or data-specific details.
Schedule C: Includes additional technical, legal, or procedural appendices.
Authorised Signatory:Formal signatures confirming approval of the agreement.
Data Protection Officers’ Statement:Confirmation from DPOs that data protection obligations have been reviewed.
